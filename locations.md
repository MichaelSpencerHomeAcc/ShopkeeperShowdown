# Shopkeeper Showdown — Locations & Actions

The board has 6 locations. On each of their 3 actions per turn, a player visits a location and resolves ONE of that location's available actions. Players may revisit the same location across multiple actions in a turn.

---

## Selling to Visitors

Three Visitors are face-up at all times. Each wants a set of resources (e.g. "2 ARM, 1 ANY") and offers two **contribution prizes**, printed on its card:

- **1st prize** goes to the player who sold the most resources into that Visitor.
- **2nd prize** goes to the runner-up.
- Ties go to whoever reached that number of sales first.
- Prizes are paid the moment the Visitor's demand is fully met. The Visitor is then discarded and replaced.

Prizes are Coins, Rep (your choice of type), Refresh, Take (from the Flea Market), Draw, Steal or Break. Large Visitors pay bigger prizes than Small ones, and a 2nd prize is never worth more than the 1st. Steal 2 and Break 2 are resolved one at a time, so you can hit two different players.

There are three ways to sell into a Visitor. Each card must match something the Visitor still needs:

1. **Sell Phase** (start of your turn, from round 2): sell any of your window cards, **up to 2 per Visitor**. You get each card's printed value plus any Rep on it.
2. **Auction** (Tavern): roll d6 for one hoard or window card and, if it fits, sell it into a Visitor of your choice.
3. **Town Crier** (Guildhall): after placing a new Visitor, sell up to 2 hoard or window cards into it at their printed value.

---

## Guildhall

**Hire a Professional**
Resolve one of the 3 face-up Professionals at the Guildhall.

**Consultation**
Spend 1 resource from your hoard. Gain 1 Reputation token of that resource's type and 2 coins. Counterfeits can't be spent this way.

**Town Crier**
Look at the top 3 Visitor cards. Place 1 of them into play, replacing a current Visitor of your choice (the replaced Visitor is discarded). Put the other 2 on the bottom of the Visitor deck. Then you may sell up to 2 resources from your hoard and/or windows into the Visitor you placed, for their printed value plus any Rep on them. The sales count toward its contribution prizes.

---

## Tavern

**Rest**
Ready all your expended Active tokens (flip them face-up), then Repair 1 of your windows.

**Auction 1**
Choose 1 resource from your hoard or windows, and optionally a Visitor that still needs it. Roll d6 and gain half that many coins, rounded up (1–3). If the resource has a Reputation icon, gain the matching Rep token. If you chose a Visitor, the resource is sold into it and counts toward its contribution prizes; otherwise it goes to the discard pile.

**Trade 3**
Swap up to 3 of your resources (from windows or hoard) with the same number of resources from the Flea Market.

---

## Wilderness

**Gather**
Roll your d6. Draw that many resources from the top of the resource deck.

**Forage 2**
Look at the top 4 resources of the discard pile. Keep 2. Return the rest in any order on top of the discard pile.

**Quest**
Name a Reputation type, then roll 2d6. The Warlock may Twist one of the two dice (on anyone's Quest, including their own).

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 2–4 | **Ambushed:** discard a random card from your hoard |
| 5–6 | **Supplies:** draw 3 resources |
| 7–8 | **Treasure:** gain 4 coins and draw 1 resource |
| 9–10 | **Trophy:** gain 2 Rep of the named type |
| 11–12 | **Legend:** gain 3 Rep of the named type and 2 coins |

## Barracks

**Report the Crime**
Name a player holding Stolen resources. They discard 1 Stolen resource from their Hoard (their choice) to the discard pile, and you gain 1 Reputation token of your choice. Counterfeits cannot be discarded this way.

**Fortify**
Repair ALL your Broken windows and move the Night Watcher badge to yourself.

**Recover Goods**
Take 1 Stolen resource from another player's Hoard into yours. It stays Stolen (so it can be Fenced, or Reported). The Night Watcher badge holder cannot be targeted.

---

## Workshop

**Take 2**
Take up to 2 resources from the Flea Market into your hoard. The Flea Market refills from the deck immediately.

**Craft**
Two Work Orders are face-up on the board, and any player may complete either of them. Choose one public Work Order whose recipe you can meet, spend the listed resources from your windows/hoard, and gain the printed coin value immediately. The completed Work Order goes to the bottom of the Work Order deck. Its slot stays empty for the rest of the round and is refilled from the top of the deck at the start of the next round.

**Appraise 2**
Look at the top 4 resources of the resource deck. Keep up to 2. Put the rest on the bottom of the deck.

---

## Thieves' Guild

**Steal 1 / Break 1**
Choose ONE:
- Steal 1: Take 1 resource at random from a target player's Hoard. Mark it as Stolen.
- Break 1: Mark 1 of another player's windows with a Broken token.

Note: The Night Watcher badge holder cannot be targeted by this action.

**Fence**
Sell 1 Stolen resource from your Hoard to the discard pile. The resource type must be DIFFERENT from the last resource fenced at the Thieves' Guild (it stays face-up there). Gain **double** the coin value of the sold resource. Ignore any Reputation icon. The Stolen marker is removed on sale.

**Launder 3**
Draw 3 resources from the top of the resource deck. Mark them all as Stolen.
